??? quote "Biography"

    Paul François is a leading bioinformatician, a Full Professor at the Université de Montréal, an Adjunct Professor at McGill University, and an Associate Academic Member of Mila. He applies computational methods, including machine learning, to unravel the mysteries of evolution, embryonic development, and immunology. In essence, he simplifies complex biological questions with mathematical models.
