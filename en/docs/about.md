# About Us

MonBUG (Montreal Bioinformatics User Group) is an association of researchers, other professionals and students in the Greater Montreal area who have an interest in the field of bioinformatics, computational biology, and data sciences. MonBUG meets every month, from September through April. Research presentations by bioinformatics leaders, students and industry representatives are followed by networking over snacks and refreshments

[Regular in-person monthly meetings](./schedule.md) will be held monthly on wednesdays from **17h30 to 19h30** and are free and open to all. Please refer to the [Schedule](./schedule.md) page for specific details regarding speaker location and room information.

Visit our [Canadian Bioinformatics Hub affiliate partners](https://bioinformatics.ca/resources/current-partners/)
