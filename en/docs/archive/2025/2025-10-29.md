**Speaker:** David Bujold

**Affiliation:** Head of the Data Unit, Canadian Centre for Computational Genomics (C3G)

**Talk Title:** The Pan-Canadian Genome Library: Harnessing GA4GH Standards for Secure, FAIR, and Federated Genomics

**Abstract:**

This talk introduces the Pan-Canadian Genome Library (PCGL), a national effort to improve how genomic research data is managed, archived, and shared across Canada. We will cover how PCGL uses standards from the Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH), including Beacon and the Data Use Ontology, to make data easier to find, access, and use responsibly. The presentation will outline how, by building on these global standards, PCGL can help researchers work together more effectively, supporting both research and clinical applications.
