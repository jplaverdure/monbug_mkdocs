# Sponsorship

MonBUG is possible through the work of volunteers, however we welcome support in order to bring in outside speakers and host other significant events.

If you are interested in sponsoring our events (a single event, a term, or a year), please [contact us](./contact.md).

MonBUG welcomes bioinformatics-related companies to present short (maximum 10 minutes) presentations before MonBUG meetings. Please [contact us](./contact.md) for more details.
