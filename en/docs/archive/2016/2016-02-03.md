# February - Genomic analyses with R: Mathieu Lajoie, Stéphane Mbatchou, Alexis Blanchet-Cohen

!!! info "Event Details"

    **Date/Time:**

    Wednesday, February 3rd, 2016 :material-clock: 16h00 - 18h00

    **Location:**

    :material-map-marker: **Location:** Jacques-Genest auditorium, IRCM

    **Schedule:**

    :material-clock: 16h00 - 16h05 — Introduction (Alain Bateman)  
    :material-clock: 16h05 - 16h40 — **Mathieu Lajoie, PhD** — an overview of three important packages for genomic analyses, GenomicRanges, rtracklayer and knitr  
    :material-clock: 16h40 - 17h00 — Break with snacks and beverages  
    :material-clock: 17h00 - 17h40 — **Stéphane Mbatchou**, co-developer of DataSHIELD, "an R library that enables the remote and non-disclosive analysis of sensitive research data"  
    :material-clock: 17h40 - 18h00 — **Alexis Blanchet-Cohen**, developer of abcrnaseqplots, an alternative to CummeRbund to analyze RNA-Seq data  

We're excited to collaborate with the Montreal R User Group on a special event, focused on genomic analyses with R.

Prizes for the speakers are generously provided by the Montreal R User Group.
