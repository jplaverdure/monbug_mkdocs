**Speaker:** Pedro Bordignon

**Affiliation:** CITADEL, CHUM’s Centre for the Integration and Analysis of Medical Data

**Talk Title:** A bioinformatics journey and CITADEL

**Abstract:**

In this talk, we will explore the speaker’s bioinformatics journey, from growing up and working in Montreal to their current role at CITADEL, the CHUM’s Centre for the Integration and Analysis of Medical Data. Through this personal and professional narrative, we will also delve into the mission and activities of CITADEL, highlighting its role in integrating and analyzing medical data to drive advancements in healthcare research.
