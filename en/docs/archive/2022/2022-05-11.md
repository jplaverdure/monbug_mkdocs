**Speaker:** Dr. Virginie Calderon

**Affiliation:** IRCM Bioinformatics Core Facility

**Talk Title:** How to single cell?

**Abstract:**

This training will give you an overview of single cell experiment and the different protocols (scRNA, snATAC, VDJ, surface protein, cell hashing…). We will deepen scRNA-seq analysis from quality control, differential expression to pseudotime/velocity.
